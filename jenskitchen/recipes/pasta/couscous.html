Couscous with Peas and Mint

This light dish is a great side for summer meals.

  • 1 c. couscous
  • 1 c. chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1/2 c. frozen peas, thawed
  • 2 T. fresh mint, chopped
  • 1 T. olive oil
  1. Bring broth to a boil in a medium saucepan.
  2. Stir in couscous, cover, and remove from heat. Let stand for 5 minutes.
  3. Fluff with a fork and stir in peas, mint, and olive oil.
