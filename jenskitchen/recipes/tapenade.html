Tapenade (Olive Spread)

This is a really easy dish to prepare and it always gets rave reviews. Make it a day ahead to allow the flavors to blend.

  • 1 8oz. jar sun-dried tomatoes packed in oil, drained and chopped
  • 1 6oz. can pitted black olives, drained
  • 1 6oz. can small green olives, drained
  • 1 c. capers, drained
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 c. olive oil
  1. In a food processor, combine all ingredients and blend until smooth.
  2. Serve with crusty bread or crackers.
