Our Story

Ube & Alfajor Café started in 2019 when two college roommates, Mia Santos and Valentina Ferrari, realized their grandmothers made two of the best desserts they had ever tasted — and decided the world needed to try both under one roof.

Two Grandmothers, One Café

Mia grew up in Manila watching her lola simmer purple yam into ube halaya for special occasions. Valentina grew up in Buenos Aires learning to fold dulce de leche into her abuela’s alfajor dough. When they opened their first small counter, they decided not to choose one tradition over the other.

Why We Do This

  • To keep two family recipes alive and share them with our neighborhood
  • To make every dessert from scratch, with no shortcuts
  • To create a warm, welcoming space for the community to gather

Our Ingredients

We source our purple yam and dulce de leche from small importers who work directly with growers. To learn more about fair-trade sourcing practices, visit Fairtrade International, which opens in a new window.
